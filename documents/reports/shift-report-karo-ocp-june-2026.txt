OBSERVATION 2 — HIGH PRIORITY
Area: Haul Road Junction — Junction of Main Haul Road and Bench 3 Access
Issue: Two contract workers (vehicle attendants) observed at 23:45 hours without dust masks (FFP2) during haul road maintenance activities adjacent to the main road. Activities involved dry sweeping of road surface and loose material removal.
Worker IDs: Contractor Card C-441 and C-507.
PPE status: Safety helmets worn (compliant), reflective jackets worn (compliant), safety footwear worn (compliant). Dust masks NOT worn despite dust generating activity.
DGMS reference: DGMS Technical Note No. 09 of 2023 (DGMS-TECH-NOTE-2023-09) Section 2.2(d) requires dust masks during haul road maintenance when road watering has lapsed or dust is visually perceptible. Road watering had lapsed for approximately 45 minutes at the time of observation.
Action taken this shift: Workers immediately directed to stop activity. Dust masks issued from the shift emergency stock (2 FFP2 masks). Toolbox talk conducted at 00:05 hours covering PPE requirements for dry haul road operations. Workers resumed with masks at 00:10 hours.
Record: Non-compliance recorded in Shift Log and PPE Issue Register. Contract supervisor (Shri D.P. Banerjee) notified by phone at 00:15 hours.
Required closure: Issue replacement masks to these workers from their contractor's PPE stock. Record toolbox talk attendance with signatures. Verify PPE issuance before next shift for contracted crew.
Priority: MEDIUM — toolbox talk acknowledgement and PPE re-issue required.
