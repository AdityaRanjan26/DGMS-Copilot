PART A — SCOPE OF AUDIT

This audit covers PPE compliance in the following zones:
(a) Main haul road and vehicle movement zones at Karo OCP.
(b) CHP building and transfer point areas at Karo OCP.
(c) Workshop and maintenance areas at both Karo OCP and Amlo Project.
(d) Explosives handling and blasting preparation zones at Karo OCP.

PART B — AUDIT FINDINGS

Checklist Item 1 — Safety Helmets (All Zones)
Compliance standard: Every person beyond the entry gate shall wear a safety helmet conforming to IS 2925 with chin strap fastened.
Persons observed: 34 (Karo OCP), 18 (Amlo Project).
Compliant: 33 (Karo OCP), 18 (Amlo Project).
Non-compliant: 1 (Karo OCP) — Departmental worker at stockyard boundary, helmet present but not worn (carried in hand).
Action: Worker directed to wear helmet. Verbal warning recorded in audit register.
Overall compliance rate: 98.1%.

Checklist Item 2 — Reflective Jackets (Haul Road and Vehicle Zones)
Compliance standard: All persons in haul road and heavy vehicle zones must wear IS 15809 Class 2 minimum high-visibility reflective jacket.
Persons observed in vehicle zones: 22 (Karo OCP), 12 (Amlo Project).
Non-compliant: 0.
Overall compliance rate: 100%.

Checklist Item 3 — Safety Footwear (All Zones)
Compliance standard: Steel-toe, steel-midsole safety boots conforming to IS 1989 Part I.
Persons observed: 34 (Karo OCP), 18 (Amlo Project).
Non-compliant: 1 (Karo OCP) — Contract worker wearing standard leather shoes, not safety footwear.
Action: Worker withdrawn from site. Contractor supervisor contacted. Worker not permitted to return without compliant footwear. Non-compliance recorded in PPE audit register.
Overall compliance rate: 98.1%.

Checklist Item 4 — Dust Masks (CHP, Crusher, Haul Road Maintenance)
Compliance standard: FFP2 minimum dust respirator required during CHP operations, crusher operations, and dry haul road maintenance.
Persons observed in dust-exposure zones: 11 (CHP and Transfer Point area, Karo OCP).
Non-compliant: 2 — Two contractual workers at CHP Transfer Point 2 (TP-2) observed without dust masks during dry maintenance operations. (Workers: Contractor Cards C-441 and C-507.)
DGMS reference: DGMS Technical Note No. 09 of 2023, Section 2.2(d): Supervisors shall ensure hard hats, reflective jackets, safety shoes, eye protection, and dust masks are verified before hazardous task allocation. These workers entered TP-2 area without supervisor PPE verification.
Action: Workers immediately stopped. Dust masks issued from emergency stock (2 × FFP2 masks issued, recorded in PPE Issue Register Ref: PI-2026-188 and PI-2026-189). Toolbox talk conducted. Contractor supervisor notified.
Root cause: Contractor supervisor had not conducted pre-task PPE verification per DGMS-TECH-NOTE-2023-09 Section 3.1. The same contractor crew was noted without dust masks on the previous B shift (B shift 21 June 2026 report).
Recommended closure: (1) Repeat non-compliance notice issued to contractor. (2) Enhanced monitoring of contractor crew PPE compliance for next 5 shifts. (3) Safety Officer to co-verify PPE for contractor crew before each shift in the CHP zone for next 2 weeks.
Overall compliance rate for dust masks in applicable zones: 81.8% (9 of 11 compliant). Below the 90% threshold — triggers enhanced monitoring per CMR 2017 Regulation 8(3).

Checklist Item 5 — Eye and Face Protection (Grinding, Welding, Crusher)
Compliance standard: Safety goggles or face shield during grinding, welding, and crusher area maintenance.
Persons observed: 6 (workshop maintenance, both sites).
Non-compliant: 0.
Overall compliance rate: 100%.

Checklist Item 6 — Hearing Protection (Crusher and Drill Operations)
Compliance standard: SNR 25 dB minimum hearing protection during crusher operation and drilling.
Persons observed in high-noise zones: 5.
Non-compliant: 0.
Overall compliance rate: 100%.

Checklist Item 7 — Reflective Jacket and Helmet Compliance at Haul Road, Stockyard, CHP, and Workshop Zones
Persons entering haul road, stockyard, CHP, and workshop zones must be observed with helmet, reflective jacket, and safety footwear.
Combined assessment for Checklist Item 7:
Total persons observed in Item 7 zones: 42.
Fully compliant (helmet + reflective jacket + safety footwear): 39.
Non-compliant on one or more items: 3 (Stockyard helmet carrier, CHP dust masks ×2).
Item 7 compliance rate: 92.9%.
Note: The two CHP dust mask non-compliance instances (Contractor C-441 and C-507) are also counted in Item 7, as dust masks are required PPE in the CHP zone. These are the same two workers as in Item 4 and the Karo OCP B shift report for 22 June 2026.
Recommended corrective action per Item 7 findings: Contractor management instruction to conduct toolbox talk with all contractor crew covering PPE requirements for each specific work zone before their first entry each shift.
